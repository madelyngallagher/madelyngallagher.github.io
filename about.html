Hi! I'm Madelyn.

I'm a graphic designer and artist currently in my second year at The University of St. Thomas, studying Digital Media Arts with a minor in Strategic Communication. I'm the Design Lead for The Crest, St. Thomas' student news organization, working for them since my first year.

Alongside my interest in art and design, I love music. My favorite albums, artists, and even music videos heavily inspire my visual work. I began designing about four years ago to decorate my room. Designing posters for my walls became my introduction to graphic design, and poster design remains my favorite medium to this day.

My art is an act of self-acceptance for myself and for others, aimed at emphasizing the strength to be unapologetically oneself while living creatively.
